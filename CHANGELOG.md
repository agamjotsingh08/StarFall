# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-09

- Fixed graphics quality changing enemy randomness through particle effects.
- Fixed taps in the scenery outside the arena triggering combat controls.
- Fixed reactor cooldown caps erasing Vanguard and Falcon ship bonuses.
- Fixed pickup banners claiming an upgrade or repair when weapons/hull were already full;
  capped pickups now clearly report their score reward as salvage.
- Expanded validation to every level/ship/difficulty combination and all upgrade paths.
- Added Casual as the default and Standard as an intermediate difficulty. Existing
  installations start with Casual once after this update; later selections persist.
- Slowed Casual enemy movement and bullets, reduced formations and enemy hull, added
  a short launch grace period, and increased player hull and damage immunity.
- Added Vanguard, Falcon and Bastion selection with distinct hulls and gameplay perks.
- Added automatic attraction for nearby supplies, more frequent repair/weapon drops
  on easier modes, and a one-hull between-mission repair bonus in Casual.
- Reduced ability cooldowns on easier modes; retained Veteran and Nightmare options.
- Added portable build instructions, installation/troubleshooting guidance and
  contribution notes for a future GitHub upload.

## 0.2.0

- Added a 12-mission campaign, four bosses, Veteran/Nightmare, dash, grazing rewards,
  mission replay and upgrade choices.
- Updated typography and military sci-fi presentation.

## 0.1.0

- Initial native Android space shooter, original art/audio, touch controls, Nova,
  pause, high scores and configurable graphics.
